How the luck formula works

1. All-play every weekEach week, every team's stat in a category is compared to all 9 other teams, not just its opponent. Beating 6 of 9 in goals means you "should" win goals 67% of the time that week (ties count half).
2. Expected vs actualAdd those percentages across 8 categories and every week to get expected wins. Compare with the category wins Yahoo actually gave you.
3. Luckluck = actual − expected. +3 means about 3 more category wins than your stats earned. Near 0 means your record is honest. Goalie-minimum forfeits are scored the way Yahoo scored them.
Completed weeks only. Source: Yahoo Fantasy, GOAT POOL weekly matchups.
